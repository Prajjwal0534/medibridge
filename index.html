PRIVACY & DATA RIGHTS

Your health information should never be a mystery.

This notice explains the MediBridge limited-beta data flow, Family Profile responsibilities, mental-health separation, AI boundaries and how to make a data request.

Notice 2026-08-25-v1
Production governance gate

MediBridge remains a prototype/limited-beta candidate. Real patient or mental-health data must not be used until the operator's legal identity, grievance contact, final retention schedule and India-specific legal review are published and approved.

Information handled

Account and contact details; provider qualifications and verification documents; Family Profiles; appointments; reports and prescriptions; clinical notes; diagnostics and pharmacy requests; consent and access events; location used for nearby care; and limited technical security events.

Why it is used

To operate connected-care workflows, verify providers, show suitable nearby care, keep each family member's record separate, deliver consent-controlled access, secure the service and provide user-requested AI explanation.

Who can receive it

Only the account holder and authorised people or verified providers according to role, purpose, consent and database security rules. General medical consent does not unlock mental-health summaries. Private mental-health professional notes are provider-only.

AI and location

AI supports explanation and preparation; it does not replace a clinician or make silent diagnoses. General AI requests use an authenticated server route. GPS is used to calculate nearby care and is not intentionally persisted in browser storage.

Family and minors

The signed-in patient is the account owner; the selected Family Profile represents the person receiving care. The account owner must have lawful authority to manage that person's data. A parent or legal guardian must manage a minor's profile.

Retention and deletion

Production retention periods are not yet legally approved. During the controlled beta, only synthetic/demo data is permitted. Data-rights requests are logged below; deletion may preserve limited audit or legal records where lawfully required, and the final schedule must be published before real-data launch.

MEDIBRIDGE TERMS

Terms for the controlled prototype

Version 2026-08-25-v1 · prepared 25 August 2026.

Not approved for public production use

These operational terms are a product safeguard, not a substitute for India-specific counsel. The legal entity name, registered address, grievance officer and jurisdiction clause must be completed before collecting real health data.

Service boundary

MediBridge connects healthcare workflows. It is not an emergency dispatcher, hospital admission guarantee, insurer or autonomous medical provider.

Emergency and AI

For life-threatening danger in India, call 112 or seek emergency care. AI output may be incomplete and must not replace qualified professional judgment.

Account responsibility

Keep credentials private, use accurate information, manage only people you are authorised to represent, and promptly revoke access that is no longer required.

Provider responsibility

Professional titles, qualifications, availability and records must be accurate. Verification does not create an employment relationship or guarantee a particular outcome.

Mental-health boundary

Mental-health summaries require separate consent. Provider private notes remain separate and peer listeners are not part of MediBridge Mental Health.

Prototype limits

Use synthetic/demo data only until launch blockers are closed. Public Jitsi is disabled by default in this release until an approved video provider and privacy assessment are configured.
